# Names

Working names: **Screenshot Brain** for the app, **the Reveal** for the story feature. None of the alternatives below have been checked against App Store listings or trademarks yet; do that before choosing one.

## The app

| Name | Why it might work |
|---|---|
| **Later, Actually** | It's the pitch in two words: you said "later", and this is later. Cheeky without trying hard. |
| **Meant To** | What people say about every screenshot. Short, and it reads well as a widget title. |
| **Keen** | The app's own voice ("keen?" is the Send line). One syllable, friendly, and it suggests intent rather than storage. |
| **Hindsight** | Looking back at what you saved, with a wink. Possibly taken, so check it first. |
| **Follow Thru** | Says what the app does. Plainer than the others, but it can't be misread as a photo organiser. |

## The Reveal

Avoid "Wrapped" (Spotify), "Replay" (Apple Music) and "Recap" (already the nightly notification).

| Name | Why it might work |
|---|---|
| **The Receipt** | "Your screenshot receipt": an itemised, slightly damning list. It also fits the share card. |
| **The Debrief** | A mate sitting you down to go over what you've been up to. |
| **The Readout** | Numbers-first and a little clinical, which suits a Reveal that leads with facts. |
| **The Tally** | Short and countable. It works for monthly versions too ("October's Tally"). |
| **The Rundown** | Casual, and the 8-card story is literally a rundown. |
