## What we're taking

- **Light is the only colour.** Ground stays misty white; colour arrives as one or two soft, directional smears (00, 07, 10) or a single bloom per object (01, 03, 12), never a flat fill.
- **Motion, not blobs.** Smears are stretched along one direction with a warm leading edge and a deep body (00, 02, 10), so they read as long exposures.
- **Glass sits on the light.** Every surface is frosted and borrows the colour behind it (00, 10, 11); the action button is tinted with the current light.
- **Grain and bleed make it physical.** Faint grain over light and glass (04, 08, 10), and light that bleeds slightly past edges (04).
- **Precise data against softness.** Tiny grey labels, monospaced data rows, hairline rulers with one orange mark (01, 13).
- **The folder tab** as the object shape for items and the widget (08, 09).
- **Dark mode** keeps the same light, glowing brighter against near-black, with tiles lit from one edge (03, 05).
